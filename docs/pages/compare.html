What we're proposing

Up to three models side by side, with the rows that differ highlighted and an option to hide the rest. The compare list follows the visitor from the listing, the finder and product pages through a tray at the bottom of the screen.

Why

The live site already has compare tick boxes and a compare page, which is a good start. What's missing is any sign of what you've picked as you move around, and a quick way to see where two similar machines actually differ.
