What we're proposing

A product page that answers the three things a buyer asks before they ask anything else: is it here, roughly what does it cost, and who do I talk to. Stock names a branch, the price is a "from" figure or a monthly finance figure, and the contact is the sales team for the visitor's governorate rather than the same Alexandria branch for everyone.

Why

  • The live 302.7 CR page and its quote page both show Mantrac Amerya in Alexandria, wherever the visitor is.
  • Every product says "Price on request". That may be the right policy, but it leaves the WhatsApp and hotline teams answering the same first question all day.
  • Product addresses sit under /en-eg/api/new-products/, outside the excavators section the listing lives in.
