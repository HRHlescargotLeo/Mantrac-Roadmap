Used Cat machines

Machines that have come back to Mantrac, inspected in our workshops and graded into three tiers. Stock changes every week, so if nothing here fits, set an alert and we'll message you when it does.

What we're proposing

Used stock that can be browsed properly: a photo count, the year, hours, branch, certification tier and a price band on every card, filters by family, tier and branch, and a short explanation of what each tier covers. When there is nothing that fits, the visitor can ask to be told when something does, or offer their own machine.

Why

  • The live page lists four used machines, all track excavators, with no branch and no price. Stock this thin needs every machine to be shown well.
  • The tiers are named three different ways ("Mantrac Certified Used" is described as "Dealer Certified Used (DCU)"), and the warranty is called both "Extension Protect Plans" and "Equipment Protection Plans".
  • Used, Rental and Promotions all promote a Used & Rental app whose links return "not found" on both app stores. This page leaves it out until there is an app to send people to.
