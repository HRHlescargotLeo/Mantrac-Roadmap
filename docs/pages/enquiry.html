What we're proposing

One enquiry for everything a customer wants priced: new and used machines, rental dates, attachments and service plans, collected in a quote basket as they browse. The form asks five things, with labels, and works out where to send the enquiry from the governorate. The confirmation gives a reference, names the team and says when they'll reply. WhatsApp is offered as an equal alternative, with the basket already written into the message.

Why

  • The live contact form has 11 fields, 9 of them required, including Salutation and both Phone and Mobile Phone. The message box is the one thing that is optional.
  • Every field uses its placeholder as its label, so the label disappears as soon as someone starts typing.
  • The quote form loads from a separate marketing domain in a frame. In our checks it didn't appear at all, which is worth confirming quickly.
  • Each quote request covers one product, so a contractor pricing a fleet fills in the same details several times.
