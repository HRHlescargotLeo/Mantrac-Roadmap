Five ideas for mantracgroup.com, built so you can click through them

We reviewed the Egypt site in September and October 2026 and put together five prototypes. Each one takes a handful of the improvements we found and shows how they would work together. This version uses the Mantrac look measured from the live site, with Mantrac's own photography, so the ideas can be judged as they would appear.

Everything that should work, works. Filters filter, the quote basket remembers what you add, forms check what you type and the calculators calculate. The machines, stock, prices and dates are sample data.

How to review

  1. Use the numbers in the bar at the top to move between prototypes, or the previous and next links at the bottom of each page.
  2. Turn on Notes in the top bar to see what each prototype proposes and why, with numbered notes on how things behave. Yellow notes describe behaviour; red notes are questions for Mantrac.
  3. Try it on your phone as well. Around two thirds of web visits in Egypt are on mobile, and the contact bar at the bottom of the screen is part of the proposal.
  4. Comment on anything: what the pages do, what they say and how they look. The typeface is a stand-in for Univers, which Mantrac licenses for its own site.
  5. Anything you add to "Quote" stays for this browser session, so the prototypes can be tried as one journey.
