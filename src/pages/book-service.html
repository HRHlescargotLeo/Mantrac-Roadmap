What we're proposing

Book a service visit online in four short steps: the machine, the problem, where and when, and who to call. The request goes to the nearest workshop with a reference, and a breakdown is steered to the hotline straight away rather than left waiting in a form.

Why

Repairs and Maintenance each have pages on the live site, but the only ways to ask for a visit are the general contact form and the hotline. A booking request that already has the serial number, the hours and the location saves the first phone call, which is usually spent collecting exactly that.
