What we're proposing

A short valuation request for anyone who wants to sell a machine or trade it in against a new one: make, model, year, hours, where it is and a few photos, then a callback within a stated time. It feeds the used stock this prototype is trying to grow.

Why

The Construction page promises "End-of-lifecycle buyback", and the used tiers depend on machines coming back to Mantrac, but there is no way on the site to offer one. Owners who want to sell are some of the easiest leads Mantrac could get, and at the moment they have to phone.

What happens next

  1. The used equipment team looks at your details and photos.
  2. We call you within two working days with an indicative value.
  3. If you'd like to go ahead, a Mantrac technician inspects the machine where it is.
  4. You get a firm offer, or a trade-in figure against the machine you're buying.
