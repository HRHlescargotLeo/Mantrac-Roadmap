Mantrac near you

Find the branch for what you need, whether that is a parts counter, a workshop, the SOS lab or a sales team, then get help with parts or book a service visit.

What we're proposing

A branch finder that answers "where do I go for this?" rather than "where are your branches?". Visitors filter by what they need (a parts counter, a workshop, the SOS lab) and by governorate, and each branch shows its services, hours, whether it's open now and how to get there. A second tab gathers parts help in one place, and a My Mantrac panel points customers at the online tools they already use.

Why

  • The 13 Egypt branches sit in a list under a country filter on the Contact page. The detail is good (services, hours, WhatsApp); it is just hard to search.
  • Two branch names are misspelt on the live site: "Abu Simble" and "Marsa Allam".
  • "Shop now" on the Parts page goes to the Cat parts store under another group company's name, and the nav lists 29 parts categories that mostly describe what that store sells.
  • Service copy promises "Timely service" without saying what timely means. One measured promise would do more.
