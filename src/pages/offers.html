What we're proposing

A Promotions page that has promotions on it. Each offer has a start and end date, says what it applies to and links to its terms, and drops off the page on its end date without anyone having to remember. When there are no offers in a category the page says so, rather than leaving a blank space under a heading.

Why

  • The live Promotions page has an introduction, a panel for an app that no longer exists and contact details. No offers.
  • Every page footer invites visitors to "Get our latest promotions straight to your inbox". The sign-up asks only for an email and has no consent wording, and Egypt's data protection rules require prior consent for marketing from 1 November 2026.
