What we're proposing

Rental that starts with the dates and the place. The visitor says when and where, sees which machines are free, what the hire would cost for that period and whether delivery is possible, then sends a booking request that lands in the same quote as everything else. The wider Cat Rentals range Mantrac already runs is linked rather than hidden.

Why

  • The live rental page lists two machines, one built in 2000 and one marked both "Reserved" and "In stock", under a paragraph about having "the widest range of Cat machines readily available".
  • Mantrac has its own storefront on Caterpillar's rental site with telehandlers, compaction and aerial equipment. The rental page doesn't mention it.
  • Rental cards show no dates, rates or minimum hire, so every enquiry starts from scratch.

Rent a Cat machine

Choose your dates and where the machine will work. We'll show what's free, the hire cost for that period and the branch it would come from. Anything not listed here can usually be arranged from another branch or the wider Cat Rentals fleet.
