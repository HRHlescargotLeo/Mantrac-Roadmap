Finance and running costs

Work out roughly what a machine would cost each month, and what a newer machine could save you in fuel and maintenance. The figures are estimates to start a conversation, not offers.

What we're proposing

Two calculators in Egyptian pounds. The finance estimator takes a machine, a deposit and a term and gives an indicative monthly payment, which the visitor can send to the finance team as it stands. The running cost calculator turns Caterpillar's fuel and maintenance claims into a yearly figure using the customer's own hours and fuel price. Underneath, the finance options are named, with who qualifies and what to bring.

Why

  • The live Financing page describes "5+ options", "100% in-house teams" and "360° cost-benefit analysis" without a single term, rate or example.
  • The homepage claims "up to $6,500 total life cost saving" for the 426F2 backhoe, in US dollars, on a site for Egyptian buyers.
  • A monthly figure is often easier to publish than a cash price, and it is the number most buyers are working out anyway.
