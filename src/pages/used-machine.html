What we're proposing

A page for each used machine with its photos, the facts a buyer checks first (year, hours, serial, branch), what the tier covers, a summary of the workshop inspection and a price band. The buyer can add it to a quote, ask on WhatsApp with the machine already named, or book a viewing at the branch.

Why

The used machines on the live site have no page of their own that we could find, and the 140-point inspection is mentioned on the listing but never shown. For a buyer spending several million pounds on a second-hand machine, the inspection is most of the argument.
